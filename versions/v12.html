Polearm classes

Polearm Mastery adds P.Atk and extra targets that scale with skill level: +3 at Lv.1 (20), +6 at Lv.9 (40), +10 at Lv.45 (74). Any SA polearm adds more (+2 or +4 depending on which patch text you read). Gladiator and Tyrant don't have it; Terramancer (new dwarf mage) is an AoE caster. fact skill page · class tree

Leveling & AoE route
Fatigue budgetWhat the Fatigue window showed on 10 Sep for a fresh character: green 178.6M XP at full rate, then yellow 43.75M at 50%, then red at 2.5% with no cap; resets daily. That is far more than a party kills in a day, so the bar is not the limit, kill speed is. The Grind Meter in the same window measures your own XP/h. in-game The 10M-a-day figure in #13 does not match what the window shows.
XP level gapFull XP −5…+4; then +5: −15%, +6: −30%, +7: −50%, +8: −65%, +9: −80%, +10: −90%, +11: −95% (mirrored below). wiki
Party XPBonus 2: 110% · 3: 125% · 4: 140% · 5: 150% · 6: 180% · 7: 210% · 8: 240% · 9: 270%, split between members. From 5 members up each share is 30% of a mob's XP, so members 6–9 add kill speed at no cost to anyone's share.
DeathPvE −1% · PvP −0.2% · killed by a PK −2% · dying PK-flagged −4%. Noblesse = 20-min alchemy scroll. #11
Zone typesS 1–2 chars · SG 3–5, "social aggression" · G 6–9, stronger mobs, better rewards. +1,731 mobs added to 71 zones lvl 1–45 (Jul 2026). Locations · Gamma #7
Other XPClan Unity +3–5% with 8+ online · treasure chests: 10 XP-chests per type per day · Assist Manager 150 min/day (180 Premium) · quest XP bypasses fatigue.
Session loop Pull the biggest social packs of your bracket, keep Provoke up, BH sweeps every pack (BH within −4…+3 of the mobs and near the Warlord's level). Between pulls: quest turn-ins and repeatables, treasure chests, Assist-Manager minutes on the boxes. Sell reagent boxes and spoil; bank Coins of Magic and Kail's coins toward the Orcish Poleaxe C and Lance B recipes (the Lance needs both).
