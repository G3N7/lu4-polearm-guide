How Master Coins turn into gear

The Lu4 store sells no weapons, armor or enchant scrolls. Premium, autoloot, inventory slots, EXP boosts and cosmetic accessories only. Master Coins are a tradeable item, so the route to gear is selling MC to other players for adena, or paying a seller in MC directly. Everything below is therefore priced in adena first and converted.

S‑grade

Not in Lu4. The level cap is 75, A‑grade is the 64–75 tier, and the Lu4 weapon database and market tracker contain no S items. The "S‑Grade Sets Bonuses" wiki article belongs to the separate PC MasterWork server. Nothing to price until the cap rises.

Running a Shillien Elder box

The SE only exists alongside the Bounty Hunter if one account has Premium, because Lu4 allows a single window and Premium adds one. Put it on the BH: the spoil bonus counts only on the spoiler, and party drop bonus is weighted by damage, so a healer that never hits anything does not dilute it.

Build implication. With SE heals and Vampiric Rage, take Full Plate (its +4% Vampiric Rage effect stacks on the buff) and the offensive SA (Wide Blow or Towering Blow) rather than Health. Enchant, SA and set rules on Lu4

Enchanting

  • Weapons are safe to +3. Armor is safe to +3, one‑piece body armor to +4.
  • A failed normal scroll crystallizes the item. A failed blessed scroll resets it to +0.
  • Set enchant bonuses need every set piece at the level; "mini" D sets get none.
  • Armor gives HP from +4 (a D helmet: +5 at +4, +12, +25, +40, +60 at +8).
  • Above safe, assume Interlude‑style ~2/3 per attempt. Lu4's exact rates were not published where I could reach.

Soul crystals (SA)

  • D, C, B and A weapons take SA. Stage by tier: D 2 / 3 / 4, C 5 / 6 / 7 / 8, B 9 / 10, A 11 / 12 / 13.
  • Insertion costs gemstones of the weapon's grade: Glaive 150 D; Body Slasher 97 C, Bec de Corbin 238, Poleaxe 306, Scorpion / Widow Maker 225, Orcish Poleaxe 555; A 170 / 260 / 400.
  • Gemstone prices: D 1,350 · C 3,600–4,050 · B 12,000–13,500 · A 36,000–40,500.
  • Stage‑0 crystals come from any blacksmith; level them by killing mobs of the right level.

Caveats. Scroll, life‑stone and finished‑item prices are Gamma's market tracker because Black and White only list materials, gemstones and the MC rate.
